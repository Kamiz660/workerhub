# DESIGN.md

## Design Philosophy

The product should feel trustworthy, modern, and practical.

Users should feel:

* This is professional
* This is legitimate
* I can trust workers listed here
* Finding help is easy

Avoid designs that feel:

* Corporate
* Overly futuristic
* Crypto-like
* AI-generated
* Startup buzzword heavy

---

## Design Inspiration

Reference products:

* [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Study their:

* Search UX
* Card layouts
* Trust indicators
* Spacing
* Information hierarchy

Do not copy directly.

---

## Visual Style

Use:

* Clean layouts
* Large readable typography
* Soft shadows
* Consistent spacing
* Rounded corners
* Strong visual hierarchy

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Glassmorphism
* Heavy animations
* Neon effects
* Decorative clutter

---

## Colors

Primary goal: trust.

Prefer:

* White backgrounds
* Neutral grays
* One strong accent color

Avoid excessive color usage.

Color should guide attention, not decorate.

---

## Typography

Prioritize readability.

Prefer:

* Clear section hierarchy
* Large headings
* Comfortable body text
* Generous line spacing

Never sacrifice readability for aesthetics.

---

## Marketplace UX Rules

Users should quickly answer:

1. What service is offered?
2. Who provides it?
3. Can I trust them?
4. How do I contact them?

These answers should be visible without effort.

---

## Worker Cards

Worker cards should prioritize:

1. Photo
2. Name
3. Profession
4. Rating
5. Location
6. Experience
7. Contact action

Avoid overcrowding cards.

---

## Mobile Design

Mobile is primary.

Every screen should work beautifully on mobile before desktop refinements.

---

## AI Design Rule

When proposing UI:

Prefer proven marketplace patterns over creative experimentation.

If unsure between beautiful and clear:

Choose clear.
